AR Trading – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

AR Trading (“the app”) displays our website, ar-trading.netlify.app, inside an Android app.

Information we collect

The app itself does not collect, store, or sell any personal information, and it does not show advertising. It needs an internet connection to load the website. Product information on the website is loaded from Google Firebase. Your cart is saved on your device. When you choose to send an order, the website opens WhatsApp with your order message, and the order is sent only when you send it. Anything you enter on the website is handled by the website.

Third-party services

The website is hosted by Netlify, which may process standard technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
